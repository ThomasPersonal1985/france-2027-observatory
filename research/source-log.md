## Editorial review corrections (12 November)

- **Harris margin of error:** The project briefing and data record previously transcribed the top of the official range as ±2.9. Page 2 of the Commission des sondages notice states ±1.4 to ±3.1 points. The poll record, mirrored data file and briefing now use ±3.1.
- **Teddy Riner policy coverage:** The prior dossier said no detailed policy programme had been verified, but its wider wording understated the initial policy positions already reported from his 9 November interview. LCP’s 10 November report supports attributed early statements on pensions, wages, work, public spending, security and anti-racism while saying the fuller programme is forthcoming. New structured policy and news items remain `under_review`, are not legacy-public, and have no publication approval.

**Editorial review (12 November): Raphaël Glucksmann.** His campaign announcement on 23 August and 11 October primary result were checked against LCP’s report and Place publique’s own result notice. The campaign says he received 79,613 votes (66.17%); the primary designation is not whole-left unity or official ballot qualification. The official campaign programme page lists six broad axes and says detailed, quantified proposals will be released progressively. The campaign’s first-part 100-day environmental booklet, published 9 November, proposes €200bn of public investment over five years, an exceptional €50bn contribution on the highest wealth, energy and mobility measures, climate adaptation, and more renewable capacity alongside maintaining nuclear energy. These figures and effects are campaign claims, not independent fiscal or climate assessments. A new policy record and news item remain `under_review`, are `legacy_public: false`, and have no publication approval. The pensions record was tightened to state the 62-year reference with differentiated departures based on hardship; its implementation details remain unsettled. The CSG/wage proposal is attributed to the candidate and its inheritance threshold recorded as stated; independent costing is outstanding. No human publication approval was granted.
