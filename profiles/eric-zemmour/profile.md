## Political biography and career
Founder of Reconquête! and a presidential candidate in 2022, Éric Zemmour is a nationalist and far-right political figure whose political identity centres on immigration restriction, national identity, assimilation and opposition to what he describes as the decline of French civilisation. The 2022 programme is historical context, not automatically his 2027 platform.

## Political record
Before entering electoral politics, Zemmour was a journalist, author and television commentator. He founded Reconquête! for the 2022 presidential election and has remained a prominent figure in debates on immigration, Islam, identity and national sovereignty.

## Main policy themes
- Sharp restrictions on immigration and asylum
- Assimilationist national identity and citizenship policy
- Security, policing and justice
- Criticism of multiculturalism and political Islam
- National sovereignty and scepticism toward aspects of European integration
