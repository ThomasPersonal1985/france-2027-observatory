# Christine Lagarde | France 2027 monitored potential-candidate dossier

**Status (12 November 2026):** Potential / unconfirmed. No declaration, party nomination, or campaign team verified.
**Political affiliation:** No current party or campaign affiliation verified. Former French economy minister; current European Central Bank president.
**Political positioning:** Not classified by the Observatory. Do not infer a platform from previous ministerial decisions or ECB office.

## Why monitored

Le Monde reported on 6 November that her announced early departure from the ECB had revived speculation about a possible 2027 role among the centre and right. The same report described the candidacy as uncertain and said no campaign team was visible. This meets the project’s monitoring threshold for a potential name, but does not establish candidacy.

In a direct Euronews interview on 9 July, Lagarde said she was not a candidate. That denial remains material and must accompany later speculation. A Harris Interactive study published 10 November asked registered voters whether they could imagine voting for named personalities. It was a potential-to-vote measure, not a first-round voting-intention scenario and not evidence of a candidacy.
