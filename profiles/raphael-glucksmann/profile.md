# Raphaël Glucksmann | France 2027 dossier

**Party / movement:** Place publique  
**Campaign status (snapshot 8 November 2026):** Declared candidate; won the social-democratic primary in October 2026; participated in the 27 August MEDEF debate.  
**Ballot status:** Declared; not yet officially qualified.  
**Political orientation:** Social democrat / pro-European

## What they stand for / known policy themes
Pro-European social democracy, European sovereignty and defence, support for Ukraine, social investment, industrial policy and ecological transition. He supports reversing the 2023 pension reform and returning to 62, with detailed contribution rules still unclear.

## Programme status and evidence limits
Won the social-democratic primary in October 2026. No complete 2027 programme indexed in the 7 November snapshot.

## Campaign context
Won the social-democratic primary on 11 October with 66% in the first round, according to Le Monde. This does not imply unity across the entire left.
