# Raphaël Glucksmann | France 2027 research dossier

**Party / movement:** Place publique  
**Political family:** Social democrat / pro-European  
**Campaign status (8 November 2026):** Declared candidate; won the social-democratic primary in October 2026; participated in the 27 August MEDEF debate.  
**Ballot status:** Declared; not yet officially qualified.  
**Programme status:** No complete 2027 programme was indexed by Observatoire 2027 on 7 November 2026.

## Political biography and career
Essayist, former MEP and founder of Place publique, Raphaël Glucksmann has built a profile around social democracy, European integration, support for Ukraine and opposition to authoritarianism. He won the social-democratic primary in October 2026 according to the project’s candidate record, but this should not be mistaken for agreement across the entire French left.

## Political record
His public career includes work as an MEP and leadership of Place publique. His European Parliament activity, public statements on Russia and Ukraine, and alliance-building with parts of the Socialist Party are relevant to his political identity. The 2027 campaign platform remains distinct from his European election platform.

## Main policy themes
- Pro-European social democracy and European strategic capacity
- Support for Ukraine and a stronger European defence posture
- Industrial policy and economic sovereignty at European scale
- Social investment and progressive taxation
- Ecological transition compatible with industrial renewal

## Current, attributable positions
### Pensions | 4 October 2026 / 27 August debate context
**Evidence type:** Public statements
At the MEDEF debate he supported repealing the 2023 reform and returning the legal retirement age to 62. The workbook notes that a detailed contribution schedule had not been published in the cited statement.

### Wages and taxation | 27 August 2026
**Evidence type:** Debate reporting
At the MEDEF debate he proposed reducing CSG on wages and financing this through higher inheritance taxation on the largest estates. The distributional and revenue effects need independent costing.

### European policy | 2026
**Evidence type:** Recurring public positioning
His central political proposition is greater European capacity and cooperation, especially on defence, Ukraine and industrial strategy. Individual commitments should be linked to specific campaign documents as they appear.

## Feasibility questions
Key tests: the fiscal yield of inheritance-tax changes; the financing of social investment; the degree of EU-level agreement needed for defence and industrial proposals; and how the primary result translates into a broader left coalition.

## What remains unanswered
Full 2027 manifesto; coalition strategy beyond the primary; detailed funding for pension repeal and social measures.
