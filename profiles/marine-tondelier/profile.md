# Marine Tondelier | France 2027 research dossier

**Party / movement:** Les Écologistes  
**Political family:** Green / left  
**Campaign status (8 November 2026):** Declared candidate; Les Écologistes leader; participated in the 27 August MEDEF debate.  
**Ballot status:** Declared; not yet officially qualified.  
**Programme status:** No complete 2027 programme was indexed by Observatoire 2027 on 7 November 2026.

## Political biography and career
National secretary of Les Écologistes, Marine Tondelier has become a prominent national voice for the French Green movement. Her political identity combines ecological transition, social protection and democratic mobilisation. She participated in the 27 August 2026 MEDEF debate, where she argued that ecological policy must be connected to household purchasing power and economic transformation.

## Political record
Her political career includes local government in Hénin-Beaumont and leadership of the Green party. She has been involved in left-wing alliances and debates over whether the ecological movement should run independently or as part of a broader coalition.

## Main policy themes
- Climate action and ecological planning
- Public transport, energy renovation and lower-carbon infrastructure
- Purchasing power and social protection during the transition
- Public services and local resilience
- A more regulated approach to business activity where environmental costs are externalised

## Current, attributable positions
### Minimum wage | 27 August 2026
**Evidence type:** Debate reporting
At the MEDEF debate she proposed raising the gross monthly SMIC to €2,000, with support for small businesses. The funding mechanism and transition timetable require further detail.

### Pensions | 27 August 2026
**Evidence type:** Debate reporting
At the MEDEF debate she joined the left-wing calls to repeal the 2023 reform. The workbook notes that the detailed financing and contribution schedule were not fully specified in the cited record.

### Climate / economy | 2026
**Evidence type:** Public positioning
Her public profile centres on ecological transition and climate policy. A full 2027 programme is needed to establish the exact mix of regulation, investment, taxes and household compensation.

## Feasibility questions
Key tests: how the €2,000 SMIC would be phased in and supported for small employers; the financing of pension reform; the distributional effects of ecological taxes and regulation; and the pace of infrastructure investment.

## What remains unanswered
Complete 2027 programme; coalition strategy; precise financing of wage and pension commitments; energy mix and transition timetable.
