## Political biography and career
National secretary of the French Communist Party (PCF), Fabien Roussel has been a national political figure since his election as an MP and his 2022 presidential campaign. He announced a 2027 candidacy in September 2026. His political tradition places industrial employment, wages, public ownership and public services at the centre of economic policy.

## Political record
Roussel led the PCF through the 2022 presidential election and the NUPES period, while maintaining a distinct communist political identity. His relationship with LFI and the wider left is important to understanding his electoral strategy.

## Main policy themes
- Higher wages and purchasing power
- Industrial sovereignty, manufacturing and energy security
- Public services and public investment
- Workers’ rights and collective bargaining
- A strong role for the state in strategic sectors
