# Teddy Riner | France 2027 candidate record

**Status (12 November 2026):** Declared candidate; announced 9 November in an interview and public campaign video.
**Ballot status:** Not officially qualified.
**Affiliation recorded:** Independent / no party affiliation stated in the announcement.
**Research updated:** 12 November 2026

## Evidence note

Riner described his positioning as neither left nor right and said he wants to bring people together. On 12 November, Le Monde reported that Emmanuel Macron publicly welcomed his candidacy, saying he was “very happy” Riner would take part in the presidential debate. This is a favourable public comment, not a formal endorsement or party alliance. The same report says people close to Riner denied that the Élysée orchestrated his candidacy.

The announcement does not establish official ballot qualification; 500 valid elected-official presentations are required.

## Policy and programme

No detailed 2027 programme has been verified. Do not infer political positions from Riner’s sporting achievements or from the president’s comments.

## Campaign relationships and outstanding questions

Campaign organization, financing, formal endorsements, policy platform and sponsorship progress require further verification. Le Monde’s report mentions people associated with prior Macron teams but also records denials by people close to Riner; these reports do not establish who controls or funds the campaign.
