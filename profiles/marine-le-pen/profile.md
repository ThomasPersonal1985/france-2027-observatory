# Marine Le Pen | France 2027 dossier

**Party / movement:** Rassemblement National  
**Campaign status (snapshot 8 November 2026):** Declared candidate; announced 7 July 2026; participated in the 27 August MEDEF debate.  
**Ballot status:** Declared; eligibility remains subject to the legal process and final sponsorship validation.  
**Political orientation:** National-conservative

## What they stand for / known policy themes
Recurring RN priorities include tighter immigration and border controls, national-preference measures, public order and purchasing power, alongside criticism of aspects of EU integration. These are recurring positions, not a substitute for a complete 2027 manifesto.

## Programme status and evidence limits
No full 2027 programme established in this snapshot. Her 2026 MEDEF pensions proposal was a return to 62, with 60 for qualifying long-career workers and 42 contribution years. Legal eligibility remains a separate issue.

## Campaign context
Marine Le Pen announced her candidacy on 7 July 2026. Her appeal judgment and cassation process remain material to eligibility; do not describe her as ballot-qualified.
