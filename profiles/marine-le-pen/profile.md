# Marine Le Pen | France 2027 research dossier

**Party / movement:** Rassemblement National  
**Political family:** National-conservative  
**Campaign status (8 November 2026):** Declared candidate; announced 7 July 2026; participated in the 27 August MEDEF debate.  
**Ballot status:** Declared; eligibility remains subject to the legal process and final sponsorship validation.  
**Programme status:** No complete official 2027 programme was indexed by Observatoire 2027 in its 7 November 2026 programme snapshot. A candidate announcement is not itself a manifesto.

## Political biography and career
Long-time leader of the National Rally (RN), Marine Le Pen has been the party’s central presidential figure in 2012, 2017 and 2022. Her political project has combined a national-sovereignty message with a sustained effort to broaden the party’s appeal beyond its historic base. She announced a 2027 candidacy on 7 July 2026. The legal status of her candidacy must be tracked separately from electoral support and sponsorships; the current source record says her appeal judgment reduced the ineligibility penalty, while further legal steps remain relevant.

## Political record
Her record includes leading the RN parliamentary group and serving as an MP; she has repeatedly contested presidential elections. The party has moved from the older Front National brand to the RN and pursued a strategy commonly described as normalisation or dédiabolisation. Her programme should not be inferred wholesale from the 2022 manifesto.

## Main policy themes
- Immigration and border controls; national preference and changes to access to public benefits
- Purchasing power, household taxation and energy costs
- Public order, policing and justice
- National sovereignty and a more restrictive approach to aspects of EU integration
- Industrial protection and economic nationalism

## Current, attributable positions
### Pensions | 27 August 2026
**Evidence type:** Public statement / debate
At the 27 August 2026 MEDEF debate, she defended a return to a legal retirement age of 62, with 60 for qualifying long-career workers and 42 contribution years. The research workbook records a stated full-year cost estimate of €9bn; this is an attributed claim, not an independently validated costing.

### Public finances | 27 August 2026
**Evidence type:** Debate reporting
At the MEDEF debate she said the RN would present a €125bn savings plan before the autumn budget debate and argued for a rule limiting current-spending deficits. The headline amount needs a line-by-line costing before being treated as a verified saving.

### Economy | 2026
**Evidence type:** Recurring public positions; programme pending
RN themes include reducing production costs and regulations while combining protectionist measures with purchasing-power pledges. Exact 2027 measures and funding should be taken from a published programme, not inferred from party history.

## Feasibility questions
Key tests: whether proposed savings are specified by budget line; compatibility of national-preference measures with French constitutional and EU law; and the interaction between tax cuts, public services and deficit targets.

## What remains unanswered
The final legal status and formal ballot qualification; detailed 2027 programme; complete financing plan; precise legal design of national-preference proposals.
