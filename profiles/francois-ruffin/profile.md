# François Ruffin | France 2027 dossier

**Party / movement:** Debout!  
**Campaign status (snapshot 8 November 2026):** Declared contender; has said he is a candidate for the presidency; released 40 proposals in a book on 6 November, while saying it is not a formal programme.  
**Ballot status:** Declared contender; not yet officially qualified.  
**Political orientation:** Left

## What they stand for / known policy themes
Left-wing, labour-focused and economically interventionist themes include industrial rebuilding, domestic production, worker protections and climate transition. His November 2026 book set out 40 proposals on industrial, demographic and climate challenges, but he said it was not a formal programme.

## Programme status and evidence limits
The book “Au boulot !” is explicitly not presented by Ruffin as a manifesto. He has said he could withdraw if a viable route to defeating Marine Le Pen emerged, but not in favour of Mélenchon.

## Campaign context
Debout! deputy. Le Parisien reported on 6 November that he published 40 proposals but described the book as not being a programme; reported readiness to withdraw in the sponsorship context should be tracked separately.
