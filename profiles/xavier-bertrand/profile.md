# Xavier Bertrand | France 2027 research dossier

**Party / movement:** Nous France  
**Political family:** Centre-right  
**Campaign status (8 November 2026):** Potential contender; not confirmed as a formal candidate in the current baseline.  
**Ballot status:** Not officially qualified.  
**Programme status:** Potential contender in the 8 November baseline. No complete 2027 programme indexed by Observatoire 2027 on 7 November.

## Political biography and career
President of the Hauts-de-France regional council and founder of the Nous France movement, Xavier Bertrand is a centre-right figure with a profile rooted in regional government and social-Gaullist themes. The project’s baseline treats him as a potential contender rather than a confirmed formal candidate.

## Political record
His career includes service as a minister, MP and president of the Hauts-de-France region. He has focused on industrial employment, regional economic development, security and public services. His regional executive record provides evidence of governing priorities but is not a presidential manifesto.

## Main policy themes
- Industrial jobs and regional economic development
- Security and public order
- Public services and territorial equality
- Work, training and vocational education
- Centre-right institutional and state reform
