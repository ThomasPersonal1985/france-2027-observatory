# Édouard Philippe | France 2027 dossier

**Party / movement:** Horizons  
**Campaign status (snapshot 8 November 2026):** Declared candidate; announced 3 September 2024; participated in the 27 August MEDEF debate.  
**Ballot status:** Declared; not yet officially qualified.  
**Political orientation:** Centre-right

## What they stand for / known policy themes
Centre-right, liberal-conservative themes include fiscal discipline, economic competitiveness, public-sector reform and pension-system changes. In September 2026 he argued for moving the legal retirement age to 65 within less than ten years, with a funded pillar.

## Programme status and evidence limits
No complete 2027 programme indexed in the 7 November snapshot. Treat earlier government policy and public statements as distinct from a new campaign platform.

## Campaign context
Former prime minister and Horizons leader. Rejects a proposed right-centre primary for now; see Le Monde coverage of 3 November 2026.
