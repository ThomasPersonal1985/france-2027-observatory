## Political biography and career
Journalist, essayist and founder of the Nous sommes la France movement, Natacha Polony presents a republican and sovereigntist critique of both market liberalism and cultural fragmentation. Her work places education, national cohesion, industrial sovereignty and the role of the state at the centre of political debate.

## Political record
Her public career has primarily been in journalism, commentary and civic/political advocacy rather than elected national office. That makes direct examination of speeches, interviews and movement documents especially important; do not infer a full governing programme from media commentary.

## Main policy themes
- Education, school authority and transmission of shared knowledge
- National sovereignty and strategic autonomy
- Industrial policy and economic independence
- Republican secularism and social cohesion
- Criticism of excessive marketisation and institutional fragmentation
