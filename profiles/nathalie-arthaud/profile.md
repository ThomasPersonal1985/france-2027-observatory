## Political biography and career
Spokesperson and presidential candidate for Lutte ouvrière, Nathalie Arthaud represents a revolutionary communist and Trotskyist tradition. Her campaigns focus on the interests of wage-earners, class conflict, workers’ control and opposition to capitalism. She has been a recurring candidate in presidential elections.

## Political record
Arthaud has run in the 2012, 2017 and 2022 presidential elections and is a long-standing spokesperson for Lutte ouvrière. Her platform is built around a consistent political tradition; however, the 2027 campaign’s exact proposals should still be drawn from its current election material.

## Main policy themes
- Workers’ wages and purchasing power
- Workers’ control and opposition to capitalist ownership
- Public transparency over corporate accounts and profits
- Internationalism and opposition to nationalism
- Collective action and labour mobilisation
