## Political biography and career
Mayor of Cannes and founder of Nouvelle Énergie, David Lisnard is a liberal-conservative figure who has criticised the scale and complexity of the French state. He is an active centre-right contender and has taken part in debates about how the right should select its presidential candidate.

## Political record
His political record is most directly visible through his local executive role in Cannes and leadership of the Association of Mayors of France. He has advocated decentralisation, lower taxes, administrative simplification and a more limited state. These themes need to be translated into specific national measures before being treated as a 2027 platform.

## Main policy themes
- Lower taxes and reduced public spending
- Administrative simplification and decentralisation
- Security and stronger enforcement
- Economic liberalism and business competitiveness
- Pension reform and a larger funded component
