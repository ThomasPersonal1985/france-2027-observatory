## Political biography and career
Former economy and finance minister (2017–2024), Bruno Le Maire has extensive experience of the Macron government’s economic policy. He leads or is associated with the Une autre France project and is listed as a probable contender in the 7 November 2026 Observatoire 2027 snapshot. Programme publication does not, by itself, prove a formal presidential candidacy.

## Political record
As finance minister he was responsible for economic policy through the COVID-19 crisis, energy-price shock and the deterioration of public finances. His ministerial record is central to assessing his credibility on debt, taxation and competitiveness, but it must be distinguished from proposals made after leaving government.

## Main policy themes
- Debt and deficit reduction
- Taxation and competitiveness
- Industrial sovereignty and productive investment
- Labour-market flexibility and employment
- Institutional reform and state efficiency

### Fiscal policy | 2026
**Evidence type:** Programme / public-position tracker
His public-position profile is strongly focused on debt, deficit, taxation and institutional reform. Exact measures and their costing should be taken from the 81 written commitments rather than generalised from his ministerial record.

## Feasibility questions
Key tests: how the programme reconciles fiscal consolidation with investment; the credibility of assumptions given his period as finance minister; and the political route from a probable candidacy to formal selection.

## What remains unanswered
Whether he formally declares; how the programme is positioned relative to Macronism; independent costing; and the relationship with other centre-right candidates.
