**Party / movement:** La France insoumise  
**Campaign status (snapshot 8 November 2026):** Declared candidate; announced 3 May 2026; participated in the 27 August MEDEF debate; presented LFI programme at Le Bourget on 7 November.

**Political orientation:** Left-wing

## What they stand for / known policy themes
LFI’s 7 November 2026 programme proposes a higher net minimum wage, price controls on essentials, wage indexation, higher taxes on wealthy households, reversing the pension reform and a foreign/energy-policy shift including leaving NATO’s integrated command and replacing nuclear power with renewables.
