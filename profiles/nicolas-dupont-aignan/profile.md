## Political biography and career
Founder and leader of Debout la France, Nicolas Dupont-Aignan has repeatedly run for president and built a sovereigntist political project critical of deeper EU integration. He combines a national-independence message with proposals on purchasing power, public services and state authority.

## Political record
A former mayor and long-serving MP, he has run in the 2012, 2017 and 2022 presidential elections. His political trajectory includes a 2017 alliance with Marine Le Pen between the two rounds, a decision that remains important context for his political positioning.

## Main policy themes
- National sovereignty and a more limited EU role
- Industrial protection and strategic autonomy
- Purchasing power and public services
- Immigration and national identity
- Institutional and constitutional reform
