TRANSPARENCY

Sources & methodology

Every factual claim should be traceable to its source and date.

Evidence labels

  • Official: government, Constitutional Council, candidate's own programme or party document.
  • Direct statement: speech, debate, interview or social post, with date and context.
  • News report: journalistic account, attributed to the publisher and journalist where available.
  • Analysis: interpretation, kept separate from the candidate's own words.
  • Unverified: not used as an established factual claim.

Polling rules

  • Store pollster, sponsor, fieldwork dates, sample, method, question wording and candidate scenario.
  • Do not treat a tracker snapshot as an individual poll.
  • Do not treat a polling average as a poll.
  • Missing candidates are null, never zero.
